## Operational boundaries

- Existing terminal workers are recovered, not killed or silently migrated. Old
  guarded sessions must be reopened with the same history before new Bash calls.
- Already overlapping recovered sessions are retained. Only new managed roots
  are serialized. A writable idle panel conversation reserves its folder too.
- Independent external CLI processes remain observable and are sampled before
  launches. This gate cannot prevent an external process starting later, OS-level
  edits, or writes within one root's own native subagent team.
- Global discovery can read owner history but cannot start a session. Project
  managers still enforce transcript ownership independently of folder naming.
- Coworker identities, device acceptance, Slack and finance remain deferred as
  specified by the owner; this pass creates no grants or messages.
