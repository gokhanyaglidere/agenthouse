# BiBilen — enclosed working house, revision 113

## Diagnosis

Revision 112 was a collection of separately framed room rectangles connected
by exposed wooden strips. Green voids among those strips made the hallway
read as outdoors. Reusing freestanding furniture pictures and merely moving
their footprint did not create a rear-wall installation. Generic desks also
failed to identify finance, accounting and sales. The SEO grid ignored roles.

## Design direction

One enclosed building, one southern landscaped garden with a woodland side
and a social terrace. No grass or trees inside the exterior building boundary.
Material palette: oak #ba8957, plaster #eadfbe, sage #71805a, brick #aa7857,
hall limestone #c9c1ac, foliage #65844c. Pixel Georgia plaques; native pixel
agent labels and existing UI type remain. Signature: the south BiBilen entry
opens onto a tree-lined brick promenade and a planted garden terrace.

```
                 ENCLOSED BUILDING
  CEO      | Engineering A | Engineering B | Technical SEO
  -------- interior cross hall, roof boundary above --------
  Studio   | Content SEO   | Growth SEO    | Rest / Sales
  -------- interior cross hall, no grass -------------------
  Cafe     | Meeting       | Marketing    | Fitted kitchen
  Finance  | Accounting    | RECEPTION    | Games
  ====================== ENTRY ========= garden door ======
  Woodland / pond / benches ---- planted brick terrace
```

The three six-person SEO rooms use their actual role names: technical/data,
editorial/content, and search/growth. Engineering has a six-person platform
room and a five-person delivery room. Existing agent identities, seat IDs and
session binding do not change. Finance, accounting and sales remain prepared
rooms with no fabricated Claude agents.

## Installation rules

North-wall appliances and casework use an actual rear-plane sprite and start
ON the north wall tile; that sprite's upper row is a nonblocking wall zone.
No transparent top padding. The body blocks the adjacent floor rows. Every
bookcase is mounted with this rule. No arbitrary shelf in a circulation lane.
Kitchen sink, hob/oven and fridge form one continuous rear-wall installation.
No fake bitmap rotation: the new fitted sink sprite faces the correct room.

Finance has a budget/charts desk, planning board, report archive and printer.
Accounting has a ledger/receipts/calculator desk and two filing banks. Sales
has proposals, customer cards, headset and a compact visitor group. SEO rooms
have distinct analytics, editorial/calendar, and map/search wallboards; the
technical room includes a server rack. Studio includes camera and softbox.

## Garden geometry

Two straight three-tile brick walks align with building exits at columns 46
and 66. They join one straight cross-walk centered at row 67. A rectangular
seating terrace opens from that walk; no zigzag corrective slivers or giant
random stone fragments. The woodland path reaches the pond shore and benches.
Trees form clustered upper and lower groves with shrubs and flower beds beneath,
not three unrelated specimens. Planting never blocks the promenade.

## Critique before implementation

This plan retains the user's warm farm-game atmosphere but makes the building
and landscape structurally different. Interior gaps now become covered halls;
the exterior wall and south thresholds expose the inside/outside boundary.
Work rooms are sized for their occupants, and departmental tools supply the
identity previously attempted with room labels. A generated preview is not
completion: inspect the actual native scenes on mobile and desktop, including
the fitted kitchen, finance, all three SEO rooms, full indoor boundary and garden.
